Tool library FAQ

Should I choose a tool or a workflow first?

Choose the workflow first. A tool is easier to evaluate after you know the trigger, required fields, owner, approval step, and fallback path.

When is a spreadsheet enough?

A spreadsheet is enough when tracking is simple, one person owns the data, and no important follow-up is being missed. Move to a CRM when relationship history and reminders matter.

What should I compare before paying?

Compare setup difficulty, pricing after any free tier, integrations, export options, support quality, and whether the first workflow can be launched in a week.
