Zapier vs Make

Both tools automate repetitive tasks. The better choice depends on how visual, technical, and customizable you need the system to be.

ZapierMake
Best forBeginners and fast setupVisual multi-step automations
Learning curveLowerModerate
Visual builderSimpleAdvanced visual paths
Good first automationForm → email → spreadsheetMulti-step lead routing
Who should skipTechnical teams wanting deep controlPeople overwhelmed by visual logic builders

If your business is still changing every week, simplicity usually beats power.
