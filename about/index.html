About Seller Insider Hub

Seller Insider Hub was built for overwhelmed small business owners trying to figure out what AI tools are actually worth using - without needing to become a tech person first.

Founder

Hi, I'm Jennie

My background is a mix of enterprise technology, entrepreneurship, and real-world business operations.

I previously worked as a Java enterprise software engineer supporting large-scale business systems at The Walt Disney Company, where I worked on integrated systems tied to reservations, ticketing, and operational workflows used across major business environments.

Later, I transitioned into entrepreneurship - building and operating my own e-commerce and creative businesses firsthand.

That experience gave me a completely different perspective on what small business owners actually need: practical solutions, affordable tools, automation that reduces overwhelm, and systems that make sense in the real world.

Why this site exists

Most AI websites talk like everyone already understands workflows, CRMs, APIs, funnels, automations, and "AI stacks."

Real business owners are usually just trying to:

  • Stop missing leads
  • Save time
  • Answer customers faster
  • Make marketing feel less chaotic
  • Figure out what tools are actually worth paying for

The goal is not to overwhelm people with tools. The goal is to help small businesses use AI in ways that actually make their lives easier.

What makes this different

  • Plain-English explanations
  • Implementation-focused guidance
  • Tool recommendations based on real-world fit
  • Step-by-step setup guidance
  • Business-type-specific workflows
  • Practical automation ideas for nontechnical owners
  • AI stack recommendations based on real business pain points

Where Seller Insider Hub is going

Seller Insider Hub is evolving into more than just an AI tools website.

The long-term goal is to build a practical AI implementation platform where small business owners can:

  • Build customized AI tool stacks
  • Follow guided implementation workflows
  • Learn practical automations
  • Connect with other business owners solving similar challenges
  • Get peer support while implementing AI into real businesses

AI is moving fast, and most small businesses are being left overwhelmed by technical jargon and generic advice.

This platform exists to make AI implementation feel practical, approachable, and useful in the real world.
