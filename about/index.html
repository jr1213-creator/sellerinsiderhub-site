Founder story

Hi, I’m Jennie.

My background is a mix of enterprise technology, entrepreneurship, and real-world business operations.

I previously worked as a Java enterprise software engineer supporting large-scale business systems at The Walt Disney Company, where I worked on integrated systems tied to reservations, ticketing, and operational workflows used across major business environments.

Later, I transitioned into entrepreneurship, building and operating e-commerce and creative businesses firsthand. That changed how I think about software completely.

Purpose

Why this site exists

Most AI websites assume business owners already understand workflows, CRMs, automations, funnels, APIs, and AI stacks. Seller Insider Hub is built for the owner who just wants to know what will actually help.

1

Stop missing leads

Show owners where calls, forms, follow-up, and intake break down before more revenue leaks out.

2

Save time

Reduce repetitive admin work so business owners can focus on selling, serving, and managing the business.

3

Answer customers faster

Help small teams respond more consistently with clearer systems, better routing, and simpler automation.

4

Make marketing less chaotic

Turn scattered content and follow-up into lighter-weight workflows that are easier to keep running.

5

Choose tools worth paying for

Compare software based on fit, effort, and usefulness instead of buying tools that create more confusion.

The goal is not to overwhelm people with tools. The goal is to help small businesses use AI in ways that actually make their lives easier.

Approach

What makes this different

A

Plain-English explanations

Pages are written for business owners who need clarity quickly, not for people looking to decode technical buzzwords.

B

Implementation-focused guidance

Recommendations are framed around what to do next, what to set up first, and what should wait.

C

Real-world tool fit

Tools are compared by usability, pricing clarity, setup effort, and workflow match instead of trend value alone.

D

Step-by-step setup guidance

Core guides connect buying decisions to implementation pages so business owners can move from research to action.

E

Business-type-specific workflows

Different business models need different systems. The site organizes recommendations around those real differences.

F

Practical automation ideas

Automation is presented as something approachable for nontechnical owners, not as an engineering project.

G

Pain-point-driven AI stacks

Recommendations start from missed calls, follow-up gaps, and content bottlenecks rather than generic software categories.

Roadmap

01

02

03

Practical automation training

Show owners how to use automations in manageable ways that improve follow-up, scheduling, and content operations.
