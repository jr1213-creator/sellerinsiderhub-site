Best path for local businesses

Calls

Use an AI phone system when missed calls cost leads or appointments.

Bookings

Use a scheduling tool when customers need an easy appointment path.

Reviews

Use a review tool when reputation and Google reviews matter.

Follow-up

Use CRM and automation when lead tracking is falling apart.

Starter stack

  • Phone system for call handling and routing.
  • Scheduling tool for bookings and reminders.
  • CRM for lead and customer history.
  • Review tool for post-service reputation workflows.

What this path does not solve

  • It does not fix customer service quality.
  • It does not replace a good local offer.
  • It does not help if the team ignores the workflow after setup.
