Popular starting questions

What should I automate first?

Start with repeated, predictable, low-risk work that has measurable value.

When is a spreadsheet enough?

Use a spreadsheet until relationship history, reminders, or pipeline visibility start slipping.

Should AI answer my calls?

Compare voicemail, human answering, AI receptionist, and hybrid call routing.

Can AI help with Etsy?

Use AI for research, drafts, and organization without inventing product facts.

Start Here FAQ

What should I do manually before adding software?

Write the process down, name the owner, list the required fields, and run it manually until the steps repeat. Software works better after the workflow is visible.

How much should I automate before it becomes risky?

Automate low-risk steps first and require human approval for money, safety, legal, medical, employment, refunds, complaints, and public replies.

What is the best first AI tool?

A general AI assistant is often the safest first tool because it can draft, summarize, organize, and improve existing work without controlling customer-facing systems.

When should I compare vendors?

Compare vendors after you know the workflow, source of truth, required integrations, approval rules, and monthly budget.
