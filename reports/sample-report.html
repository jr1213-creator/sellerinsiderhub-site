Example recommendation

Start with missed lead follow-up before adding advanced AI. The workflow is frequent, the data fields are predictable, the revenue impact is clear, and the error risk can be controlled with human review.

Sample business context

A small local service business receives inquiries from a website form, phone calls, referrals, and social messages. The owner answers quickly when available, but follow-up depends on memory and scattered notes.

Priority workflow

New inquiry
->
Structured intake
->
CRM lead
->
Callback task
->
Review weekly

Tool categories to compare

  • CRM tools for tracking leads, notes, stages, and follow-up dates.
  • Automation tools for routing form submissions and reminders.
  • Phone system tools if missed calls are part of the problem.
  • Email tools for simple post-inquiry and post-service follow-up.

Risk controls

  • Do not let AI promise prices, availability, refunds, or technical outcomes.
  • Require human review for complaints, urgent issues, and high-value opportunities.
  • Keep the CRM as the source of truth for lead status and next action.
  • Review failed automations weekly until the process is stable.

Next steps

  1. Write the intake fields.
  2. Choose the tracking system.
  3. Test the workflow manually for one week.
  4. Compare tools only after the workflow is clear.
