## Selection method

These are our three highest-priority long-tail opportunities for the newly approved GetResponse affiliate. Priority reflects audience fit, specificity of the task, proximity to a software decision, and the original help we can provide. It is not a ranking of measured monthly search volume. No paid keyword-volume, keyword-difficulty, Search Console query, or conversion dataset was available for this selection. Search-result presence establishes relevant intent and competing coverage; it does not establish traffic, attainable rankings, or commissions.

We searched broad small-business GetResponse questions, current Starter/Marketer comparisons, lead-magnet PDF delivery, and Shopify cart recovery. Narrow searches included:

- `GetResponse Starter vs Marketer for small business`
- `"GetResponse Starter vs Marketer" small business`
- `how to send a lead magnet with GetResponse`
- `GetResponse lead magnet PDF email delivery tutorial`
- `GetResponse lead magnet PDF autoresponder landing page tutorial`
- `GetResponse Shopify abandoned cart email setup`
- `Shopify GetResponse abandoned cart email setup starter marketer`

Results included current vendor pricing and help pages, broad third-party reviews and comparisons, and specific implementation guides. Secondary results were used to inspect intent and coverage; product facts were checked against primary GetResponse or Shopify documentation. Some broad results and older help pages use different plan names, trial durations, or feature descriptions. Specific current plan and workflow documentation controls our articles.
