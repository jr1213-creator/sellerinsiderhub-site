How to set up Canva so your business looks consistent

The goal is not to become a designer. The goal is to stop rebuilding every graphic from scratch and make your brand look intentional everywhere customers see you.

Best first setup

Brand kit + 5 reusable templates + one lead magnet.

This gives you speed and consistency.

Step 1: Set up your design home base

Start with Canva if you need fast, beginner-friendly graphics for social posts, PDFs, thumbnails, signs, product mockups, or simple marketing materials.

Step 2: Create only five templates first

  • Instagram/Facebook post
  • Pinterest pin
  • Lead magnet cover
  • Offer/service explainer
  • Testimonial/review graphic

Five templates you actually use are better than 50 pretty designs that never leave your drafts.

Step 3: Pair Canva with your content workflow

Use AI to draft the message, then Canva to package it visually. That is the practical stack: words first, design second.
