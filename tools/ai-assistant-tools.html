#1 - Editorial fit 4.8/5

ChatGPT

Best for: General AI assistant, planning, analysis, drafting

Why it is included: ChatGPT is included because it is the most flexible everyday assistant for planning, drafting, summarizing, and turning messy business notes into usable first drafts.

What's good

  • Flexible and strong general-purpose assistant
  • Great for prompts, strategy, copy, research synthesis

Watch out for

  • Output still needs human review
  • Can become generic without business context
