How we ranked these email marketing tools

We prioritized beginner usability, time to first useful workflow, pricing realism, integrations, setup clarity, and whether a tool solves a problem a small business owner can recognize immediately.

Ranked email marketing tools

#1 - Editorial fit 4.6/5

MailerLite

Best for: Beginners, Etsy sellers, and local businesses that need simple email follow-up

Why we ranked it here: MailerLite ranks first for beginners because it keeps the first email workflow simple: a form, a landing page, a welcome sequence, and basic automation without forcing owners into enterprise-level complexity.

What's good

  • Easy to understand
  • Good value
  • Forms, landing pages, and automations in one place

Watch out for

  • Not as deep as advanced enterprise platforms
  • Advanced segmentation is limited
